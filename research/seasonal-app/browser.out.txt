Method paragraph (canada): Experimental preview: seasonal correction. In this build each MODIS month before February 2012 is multiplied by the factor of its calendar month, not by one yearly ratio. A month’s own ratio is shrunk toward the yearly 2.10 (empirical Bayes: weight w = τ² ÷ (τ² + s²), s² = that month’s year-to-year variance, τ² = the real spread between months); a month with w below 0.5 keeps 2.10. Tested out of sample in research/seasonal.mjs (each overlap year hidden in turn): median monthly error 16.5% → 11.7% in Canada and 11.7% → 6.9% in Argentina, lower in 12 of 14 and 14 of 14 held-out years; no clear gain in small zones. It assumes the 2012–2025 monthly ratios also held in 2001–2011. Here, same test: 16.5% with one ratio, 11.7% with the factors below (165 months). Not the validated figures.
