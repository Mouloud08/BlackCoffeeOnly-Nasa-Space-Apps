## 4. Proposed project-page sentences (numbers above)
"Next step, already tested out of sample: adding a detection floor (the small fires VIIRS sees where MODIS saw nothing, estimated for each 0.5° place and calendar month) to the zone ratio lowered the error of predicted VIIRS counts in 2° boxes from 15.5% to 14.1% in Canada and from 9.9% to 9.1% in Argentina (leave-one-year-out; better in 13 and 12 of 14 years) and barely moved the national figures (Canada 2.01× → 2.04×, official area burned 1.92×; Argentina 0.68× → 0.71×). It is not in the app because it adds fire to months where MODIS recorded none (8.1% of Canada's 2003–2011 total): an assumption about the past, not a measurement."
"Ratios that vary place by place (each 0.5° place shrunk toward its zone) did not help at the 2° scale (15.5% → 15.2% in Canada, 9.9% → 9.7% in Argentina), so the app keeps one ratio per zone."
